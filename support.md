# Support

Found a problem or have a question?

**[Open a support request](https://github.com/sebcyde/cellar-docs/issues/new)**
(free GitHub account needed). We aim to reply within one business day.

Please include:

- what you were doing and what happened
- your browser
- a screenshot if you can

Don't include spreadsheet contents you consider confidential: support requests
on GitHub are public. For anything private, email
[sebcyde@hotmail.com](mailto:sebcyde@hotmail.com).

Before writing, the [FAQ](faq.md) covers the most common questions.
