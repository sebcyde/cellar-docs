# Cellar privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

This policy explains what information Cellar, a Confluence Cloud app published
by Sebastian Cyde ("we", "us"), handles and how.

## What Cellar is

Cellar adds spreadsheets to Confluence Cloud pages. It runs on Atlassian Forge,
Atlassian's app platform, and all of its code runs and stores data inside
Atlassian's cloud.

## Information Cellar stores

For each spreadsheet on a page, Cellar stores, in your Atlassian site's own Forge
storage:

- the spreadsheet's contents (cells, formulas, formatting, charts)
- its version history: the time of each save, the Atlassian account ID of the
  person who saved it, and a short description such as "Imported budget.xlsx"
- the ID of the Confluence page and of the macro on that page

Cellar reads, but does not store:

- whether the current user can edit the page, to decide whether to allow editing
- the contents of the current page, only when a user chooses to import a table
  from it

## Information Cellar does not collect

- No names, email addresses or profile details. Cellar only sees Atlassian
  account IDs.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- Files you import are processed in your browser and saved as spreadsheet
  contents; the original file is not uploaded or kept.

## Where data is processed and stored

All data stays inside Atlassian's infrastructure, in the region your Atlassian
site's data is hosted, under Atlassian's own security and data residency
controls. Cellar does not send data to any server outside Atlassian. The app is
eligible for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program.

## Who can see it

Your spreadsheets are visible to the people who can view the Confluence page
they are on. We, the developer, do not access the contents of your sheets.
Atlassian operates the infrastructure under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Closed Atlassian accounts

Each week Cellar reports the Atlassian account IDs it holds to Atlassian, using
Atlassian's personal data reporting service. When Atlassian says an account has
been closed, Cellar removes that account ID from version history. The versions
are kept, but no longer show who saved them.

## Retention and deletion

Spreadsheet data is kept while Cellar is installed. Older versions are thinned
automatically: every version from the last 24 hours, then one per hour for 30
days, then one per day. When Cellar is uninstalled, Atlassian removes the app's
stored data according to Forge's data retention rules.

## Support requests

If you contact us for support, we use your message and contact details only to
help you, and keep them for as long as needed to resolve the request. Support
requests made through GitHub Issues are public and are also subject to GitHub's
own terms and privacy policy; email us instead for anything private.

## Your rights

You can view, export and delete your spreadsheet data at any time using
Confluence and Cellar's download features.

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have
the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we
hold about you, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Cellar itself only
holds Atlassian account IDs inside your own Atlassian site; your organisation's
Confluence administrators control that data. For any privacy request, contact us
at sebcyde@hotmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK
Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
